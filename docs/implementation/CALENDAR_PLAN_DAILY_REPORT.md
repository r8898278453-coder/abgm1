# Daily calendar plan accountability

21 November 2026 IST · P09-01

The private daily dashboard and CSV count nine types of saved calendar revisions on their recorded IST day. They cover plan creation and closure, sealed requests, generation admission, saved drafts, generation failures, cancelled items, and photo attachments and removals. A retry can contribute another recorded revision.

Eight selected-day slot counts use the current plan records for that date. They show pending, generating, failed, cancelled and saved slots. Saved slots can also show a missing linked post, a retained publishing admission or a current published claim. These three are subsets of saved slots. A retained admission can come from another date. A published claim reflects recorded settlement for the linked post. The counts span open and closed plans with retained slots.

Plan, post and claim joins respect the selected client. The report exposes aggregate counts and leaves topics, captions, revision manifests, photo details and provider identifiers in their source records. Current slot states and links can change between live reads. Manual and scheduled V13 snapshots preserve the counts observed at capture. V1–V12 retain their original payloads and hashes. Public client reports continue to use their approved metric allowlist. The database schema remains 064.
